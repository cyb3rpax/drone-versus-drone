From the game to the sky

01

Build it

Two decks, four pods, one board. The build book walks you through it — no soldering, every cable plugs in.

02

Learn in the game

Fly your real route from home to Harmony in the browser first. Crashes are free there.

03

Do the free FAA test

TRUST takes about 20 minutes online and you can’t fail it. Under 250 g means no registration for recreational flights.

04

Fly it for real

Open Fly for Real on your phone, pick a mission from the board, and the GPS track pays Drone Coins into your account — 10× the game rate.

Real flying is on you: keep the drone in sight, under 400 ft, away from people, roads and airports (Gnoss Field in Novato has its own airspace), and never over Harmony while school is in. Check faa.gov/uas/recreational_flyers before you take off.
