You’re a drone courier. Fly from your own front door to Harmony Montessori in Tiburon — then make it home again on a battery that can’t quite do both.

WHAT IS THIS GAME?

You fly a delivery drone across a stretch of coast and islands, land at Harmony Montessori, and bring it back. It sounds easy. Your battery disagrees.
📍

1 · YOUR REAL ADDRESS

Type in where you actually live in Marin County. The game measures the true distance from your house to Harmony Montessori (376 Greenwood Beach Rd, Tiburon) and builds the world to match — one mile of real distance takes 30 seconds of flying.

🚁

2 · FLY THERE

Take off and cross open water, forested islands, villages and farmland. Steer with W/S/A/D and the arrow keys, climb with SPACE, drop with SHIFT, and press C to switch between the chase camera and a first-person nose cam.

🔋

3 · WATCH THE BATTERY

One charge covers roughly half your trip. The HUD shows miles remaining and your estimated range, so you always know whether you’ll make it. Run out over the water and you ditch — mission failed.

🔌

4 · RECHARGE AT HARMONY

Land on Harmony’s pad, top the battery up, then turn around and fly home. One glowing field battery floats somewhere along the route each run — grab it for an instant free full charge.

🪙

5 · GET PAID

Touch down back home to bank 100 Drone Coins. Every completed round trip pays, and your balance is saved to your pilot account forever.

👑

6 · BUY BETTER DRONES

Spend coins in the hangar on five aircraft — Sparrow, Swift, Mule, Falcon and Titan — plus six paint jobs. Each drone is faster, tougher and longer-ranged than the last.

WHAT MAKES IT HARD

Everything in the sky wants a piece of your drone.
🌲

THE WORLD IS SOLID

Towering pines, hills, houses, apartment blocks and sea rocks all have real collision. Clip one gently and you bounce. Hit one fast and something breaks.

🐦

BIRDS FLY AT YOUR ALTITUDE

Flocks patrol the route and drift to whatever height you’re at — you can’t climb over them or duck under. You have to steer around or thread the formation.

🔧

CRASHES BREAK REAL PARTS

Real parts, real fixes: a bird chips a propeller, a belly-flop knocks a silicone bumper loose, a nose-first hit shakes the front-sensor bolts out. You fix it with the exact right part and bolt.

🛠

YOU DO THE REPAIRS

Broken drones fly at a crawl. Limp to the parts shop, buy the replacement, then fit it yourself: drag each screw onto the right mount on your drone and turn it tight with the arrow keys.

THE RULES OF THE SKY

Simple to learn. Unforgiving to ignore.
  1. Pilot account required. Register or log in every session — your coins, drones and record belong to your account.
  2. One battery per leg. It drains the whole flight and covers only about half the round trip. Watch the range estimate against the miles to go.
  3. Recharge on pads only. Your home pad and Harmony’s pad are your lifelines. Run dry over open water and you ditch at sea.
  4. The round trip pays, not the halfway point. Reach Harmony, top up, fly home — then bank your 🪙100.
  5. Terrain wins every argument. Any hit above your drone’s crash tolerance breaks a part.
  6. Broken drones limp. A fraction of your speed, treetop height — just enough to reach the parts shop.
  7. You fix it yourself. Buy the part (on credit if you’re broke), drag each screw to its mount, torque it with the arrows.
  8. Spend wisely. New drones and paint jobs are permanent on your account — earn your way up to the TITAN.
